Campus Entrance 1 to Teva

Floor Campus level
Walking time 8-10 min
Mode Video + steps

Leave Entrance 1

Exit the security booth at Entrance 1 and immediately go up the first flight of stairs.

2

Continue to the upper staircase

Continue straight for about 250 meters until you reach another staircase on the left, with a path descending toward the main road on the right. Take the staircase going up.

3

Cross the bridge

Continue for another 250 meters until you reach a bridge and cross it. After crossing the bridge, veer left into the plaza area.

4

Move alongside the building

At the entrance to the plaza, turn left and walk alongside the building until you reach a staircase going down.

5

Pass the International School

Go down the staircase and continue along the path, passing the International School on your left.

6

Arrive at the building

At the next intersection, veer right and stay on the path until you reach the building.
